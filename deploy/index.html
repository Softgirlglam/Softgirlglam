Makeup training

Learn the art.
Master your craft.

At The Soft Girl Glam, we believe makeup is more than beauty—it’s a skill, an art and a powerful form of expression.

Our makeup trainings are designed for aspiring and growing makeup artists who want to build a strong foundation, refine their techniques and develop the confidence to create flawless, professional looks.

Through practical, hands-on training, you’ll learn how to understand different face shapes and features, perfect your complexion work, create seamless blends, work with different skin types and tones, and develop techniques that translate beautifully from the makeup chair to real clients.

Whether you’re picking up your first brush or looking to elevate your existing skills, our classes are structured to meet you where you are and take your artistry to the next level.
